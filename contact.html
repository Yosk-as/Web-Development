COD: Black Ops 6 Season 1 Meta

Recent Update: Omnimovement & Warzone Integration

The launch of Season 1 has officially brought Omnimovement to Warzone. This allows players to sprint, slide, and dive in any direction (360 degrees), completely changing how gunfights occur in the Area 99 map. Mobility is now the most important stat for any viable build.

Regarding the weapon meta, the most recent balancing patch has significantly reduced the "visual recoil" on Assault Rifles, making the XM4 and Ames 85 feel nearly hitscan at medium ranges. In competitive Ranked Play, we are seeing a 3-stack SMG meta on smaller maps to capitalize on the faster dive speeds.
