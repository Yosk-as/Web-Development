Counter-Strike 2: The Return of Cache

Competitive Impact: Cache Re-entry

The legendary map Cache has finally been added back into the active duty rotation for competitive play. This version features a complete visual overhaul designed for the Source 2 engine, removing the "overgrown" green aesthetic for better player visibility.

For competitive play, the return of Cache shifts the focus back to Mid-Control. Teams that can dominate the middle of the map gain instant access to both A-shroud and B-vent, forcing CT sides to play more aggressively than they do on maps like Mirage or Inferno. This has already led to a surge in double-AWP setups during professional scrims.
