Valorant Patch 9.11 Meta Analysis

Neon & Shotgun Nerf Breakdown

According to the latest Official Valorant News, high-mobility aggression has been heavily targeted. Neon's High Gear slide now has a much wider error window, stopping "pixel-perfect" instant headshots. Furthermore, Shotguns (Judge and Bucky) have seen a pellet spread increase while jumping, shifting the meta back toward tactical rifling.
