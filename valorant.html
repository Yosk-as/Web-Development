Valorant Patch 9.11 Meta Analysis

Neon & Shotgun Nerf Breakdown

The latest update has heavily targeted high-mobility aggression. Neon's High Gear has received a significant nerf to its slide accuracy, forcing players to commit to movement rather than using it as a reactive combat tool. The sliding friction has been increased, meaning you can no longer "pixel-perfect" stop for an instant headshot.

Additionally, Shotguns (Judge and Bucky) have seen a pellet spread increase while jumping and sliding. This change directly impacts "dive" compositions, making it much harder for agents like Jett or Raze to find reliable one-tap kills while mid-air, shifting the meta back toward traditional rifling and patient site takes.
